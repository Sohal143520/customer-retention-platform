## Current change

Follow-up disposition is stored on each retention action and returned with its actor/timestamp audit history. `RETAINED` cannot be recorded until 90 days after action assignment; `CHURNED` may be recorded when observed. An authenticated summary endpoint reports recorded outcomes. Historical comparison baselines can be uploaded separately as 90-day segment aggregates. Bulk model scores are saved with timestamps on customer records; a retention action copies its segment and immutable score snapshot at assignment. Assignment requires a backend-generated score at least 50%, no older than 24 hours, and a customer who has not already churned. The authenticated bulk playbook endpoint explicitly assigns deterministic recommendations to uncovered eligible active customers; it does not send customer communications. The comparison uses the first action per customer in a selected 90-day window, and withholds rates when follow-up is immature, segment customer counts differ, score eligibility is unverified, or an outcome is pending. It reports an observed relative change only; the before/after result does not establish causality. The dashboard also reports churn recall on loaded labels at the 40% screening threshold; this is not an independent holdout result unless a separate evaluation dataset is used. Existing action rows have no backfilled risk snapshots.

The baseline CSV schema is `Period_Start,Period_End,Customer_Segment,High_Risk_Customers,Churned_Within_90D`. Each file represents one 90-day inclusive historical period, with one unique row per segment. A repeated upload replaces only that same period's baseline rows; invalid files leave saved rows unchanged.
